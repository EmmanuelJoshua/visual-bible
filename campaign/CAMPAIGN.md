# Visual Bible: "Step Into the Story" social campaign

**Hero asset:** a 30-second motion-graphics film made in HyperFrames from real footage of the app.
- Vertical cut: `renders/visual-bible-vertical.mp4` (1080×1920), for TikTok, Reels, Shorts and Stories.
- Wide cut: `renders/visual-bible-wide.mp4` (1920×1080), for X, LinkedIn and YouTube.

To re-render either cut, see [README.md](README.md).

`[link]` is a placeholder for the app's public URL, which the repo doesn't record. Replace it before posting.

---

## Hook

> **You've read the story. Now walk into it.**

Alternates for A/B tests:
- "The whole Bible, as one world you can walk into."
- "Paul's journeys, Babylon's gates, Solomon's temple, all on one map."

## Key messages

1. **One world, one timeline.** The biblical world is a hand-painted 3D relief, from Rome to Persia. Drag the ages along the bottom and the world changes with them.
2. **Zoom from the map into the land.** From afar it's a museum model on a plinth. Up close you're among the trees, hills and water, with 160+ cities and towns rebuilt from history (Antioch, Babylon, Jerusalem) that rise, change and fall with the year.
3. **Follow the journeys.** Thirteen journeys, from Noah and Abraham through the Exodus and the Ministry of Jesus to Paul's voyages, play out on the map. The camera follows the route stop by stop.
4. **Build it as written.** Solomon's temple, the tabernacle, Noah's ark and more go up part by part in the order the text gives, with the verse beside every step. The temple has 17 steps.

Supporting proof points, for replies and comments:
- 449 dated events and about 3,000 people in the family tree.
- Story mode: a narrated path through the same world that you can pause anytime.
- Nothing to install: it runs in the browser on desktop and phone.
